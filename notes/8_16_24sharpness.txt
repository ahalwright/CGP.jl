A flat (or wide) fitness minimum has the same fitness value over a wide range of parameter values.

This is in contrast to a sharp minimum where only a small range of fitness values give the minimum value.

Perhaps the opposite of sharpness is robustness.

The emphasis in optimization has been to minimize sharpness as you can see from the titles of the above  papers.

I propose to consider sharpness in cultural evolution.  It is not clear that sharpness is always bad in cultural evolution.

Human ingenuity is good at coming up with very specialized sharp solutions which are useful.

Hypothesis:  Biological seldom finds sharp solutions.
